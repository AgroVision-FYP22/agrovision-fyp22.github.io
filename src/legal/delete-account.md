You can delete your Croptimum account and everything you created in it.

## In the app (fastest)

1. Open **Account** (tap your picture on the Home screen).
2. Tap **Delete account**.
3. Enter your password and confirm.

Your account is deleted straight away.

## If you can't use the app

Email [vihangamunasinghe.22@cse.mrt.ac.lk](mailto:vihangamunasinghe.22@cse.mrt.ac.lk) from the address you registered with, with the subject
"Delete my account". We will confirm your identity by replying to that address and delete
your account within **30 days** (usually much sooner).

## What is deleted

- Your account, profile, name, email, phone number and password
- Your farm fields and their locations
- Your chats with the assistant, including photos and the assistant's memory of them
- Your community posts, comments, votes and blocked-user list
- Your equipment and shop listings, photos and reservation requests
- Your pest and disease reports
- The reports you made about other content
- Your photos stored with our image provider (Cloudinary)

## What may remain

- **Backups**: deleted data stays in our encrypted backups until they are replaced, ⚠ CONFIRM: backup retention (for example up to 35 days). It is not used for anything else and is not restored except after a disaster, in which case your deletion is repeated.
- **Government notices you posted** (officers only) stay, without your name, because they are public notices.
- Records we must keep by law, if any.
- Anonymous, combined statistics (for example the number of reports in an area) that no longer identify you.

If you only want to remove some content, delete the individual post, comment, listing or
conversation in the app instead.
