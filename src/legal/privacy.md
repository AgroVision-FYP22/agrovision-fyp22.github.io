Croptimum is an app that helps farmers in Sri Lanka with crop advice, weather, market
prices, equipment sharing and a farming community. This policy explains what personal data
we collect, why, who receives it, how long we keep it and what you can do about it.

## 1. Who we are

The controller of your personal data is **Croptimum**
("we", "us"). Contact us about anything in this policy, or to use your rights:

- Email: [vihangamunasinghe.22@cse.mrt.ac.lk](mailto:vihangamunasinghe.22@cse.mrt.ac.lk)
- Data protection contact: Croptimum privacy team

## 2. What we collect, and why

| What | When | Why we use it |
|---|---|---|
| **Account**: first and last name, email, password (stored only as a one-way hash), optional phone number, optional display name, role (farmer, officer, agronomist, equipment owner) | When you register or edit your profile | To create and secure your account, and to show your name on your posts and listings |
| **Consent record**: the terms version you accepted, that you said you are 18 or older, that you agreed to processing outside Sri Lanka, and when | At registration, and when the terms change | To prove you were informed and agreed |
| **Farm fields**: field name, crop, planting date, location (map pin) | When you add a field | To give advice and weather for your land |
| **Your questions to the assistant**: text, photos you attach, voice recordings you make, and the assistant's answers | When you chat | To answer you |
| **Community content**: posts, comments, votes, photos, and the people you block | When you use the community | To run the community |
| **Listings and reservations**: equipment and shop listings (title, description, price, region, photos, contact phone) and reservation requests | When you list or reserve | To let farmers rent and buy from each other |
| **Pest/disease reports**: what you saw, where, when, optional link to a chat | When you report an incident | To warn nearby farmers (the heatmap shows areas, not you) |
| **Device location** | Only when you tap a button that needs it (for example "use my current location") | To fill in a field or report location. We do not track you in the background |
| **Reports you make** about content or assistant answers | When you report | To keep the community and the assistant safe |
| **Crash reports** (technical details about an error and your device, never your messages or farm data) | **Only if you allow it** in the app (Account → Share crash reports) | To fix bugs |
| **Technical data**: IP address and request logs on our servers | Automatically | Security and fixing problems |

We do **not** ask for payment details, your contacts, or your National Identity Card number,
and the app does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

## 3. Our legal basis

- **Consent**, which you give at sign-up (and can withdraw, see section 9), for the
  processing of your questions, photos and voice by AI and storage services outside Sri
  Lanka, and for optional crash reports.
- **Performance of our service to you**, for running your account, fields, community,
  listings and reservations.
- **Our legitimate interest** in keeping the service secure and free of abuse (logs, reports
  and moderation).
- **Legal obligation**, where the law requires us to keep or give information.

## 4. Who receives your data

We use these service providers. They process data for us and only for these purposes.

| Provider | What they get | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Google Cloud (Cloud Run)** | Hosts our app servers, so all app data passes through them | Mumbai, India (asia-south1) |
| **Supabase** | Stores the database (accounts, fields, chats, posts, listings) | Mumbai, India |
| **Google (Gemini API)** | The text of your questions, photos you attach, and the field facts needed to answer (crop, location). Used to generate the assistant's replies. We use Gemini under paid terms, under which your content is not used to train Google's models | Google's servers; the region is not fixed by us |
| **Google (Gemini API)**, speech to text | Your voice recording, to turn it into text. The recording is not kept after transcription | Google's servers; the region is not fixed by us |
| **Cloudinary** | Photos you upload (chat, posts, listings), served back through a link | Cloudinary's cloud storage; Cloudinary chooses the location |
| **Open-Meteo** | The coordinates of the field you ask weather for (no name or account) | Europe |
| **Google Maps** | Map tiles when you pick a field location; Google receives the map area you view | Global |

Other users can see what you choose to publish: your name (or display name), role badge,
posts, comments and listings (including the contact phone you add to a listing).
Government officers can see the same. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

## 5. Sending data outside Sri Lanka

Our servers and the providers above are outside Sri Lanka. By ticking the box at sign-up
you agree to your questions, photos and voice recordings being processed there. We choose
providers that apply security and confidentiality obligations to your data, and we keep to
the transfer rules of Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act. If you do not agree you
cannot use the assistant, because it cannot answer without these services; you can delete
your account (section 9).

## 6. The assistant is automated advice

The assistant's answers are generated by AI and checked by automated safety rules. **They
can be wrong.** Before using chemicals, check with your agricultural officer. We do not use
the assistant to make decisions about you that have legal or similar effects. If you think
an answer is wrong or harmful, use "Report this answer" under it.

## 7. How long we keep data

- **Account, fields, posts, comments, listings, reports you filed**: until you delete them or your account.
- **Chat**: a conversation stays open for 30 days after your last message. After that you can
  still read it for up to one year, then we delete the whole conversation, its messages and
  its saved reasoning.
- **Reports about content**: deleted 180 days after they are resolved (reports about assistant answers, 180 days after you filed them).
- **Login sessions**: end after at most 180 days, or when you log out.
- **Server logs**: 30 days.
- **Backups**: we do not keep separate backups of the database, so deleted data is gone as soon as it is deleted.

## 8. Security

Connections between the app and our servers are encrypted (HTTPS). Passwords are stored as
one-way hashes. Login tokens are kept in your phone's secure storage. Access to our systems
is limited to people who need it. No system is perfectly safe: if a breach is likely to
harm you we will tell you and the Data Protection Authority as the law requires.

## 9. Your rights and choices

You can, at any time:

- **See and correct** your details: Account → Edit profile.
- **Withdraw consent** for crash reports: Account → Share crash reports.
- **Delete your account and everything you created**: Account → Delete account. This
  permanently removes your account, fields, chats, photos, posts, comments, votes,
  listings, reservations and reports, including the copies held by our providers
  (photos at Cloudinary, the assistant's conversation memory). Nothing is kept except
  where the law requires. You can also follow the
  [account deletion instructions](/delete-account) on the web.
- **Block people** and **report content**: from any post or comment.
- **Ask us** for a copy of your data, to object to some processing, or to restrict it,
  by writing to the address in section 1. We reply within one month.
- **Complain** to the Data Protection Authority of Sri Lanka if you think we have not
  handled your data properly.

Withdrawing consent to processing outside Sri Lanka means we cannot provide the
assistant; delete your account if you want us to stop completely.

## 10. Children

Croptimum is for people aged **18 or older**. We do not knowingly collect data from
children. If you think a child has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

## 11. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters, we will change the version and ask you to
accept it again in the app before you continue to post or chat.
